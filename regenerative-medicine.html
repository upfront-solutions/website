Common questions

Why can't regenerative clinics run Google Ads?

Google bans promotion of speculative and experimental treatments, including most stem cell, cellular, gene therapy and PRP, outside of FDA-licensed products or purely educational ads. For most independent practices, paid search for your core treatments simply isn't available.

How fast does it work?

Local visibility can move in weeks. Treatment pages build over 60 to 120 days. You get monthly progress in plain English, and we're month to month, so we keep earning it.

Do you work with clinics like ours?

We specialize in regenerative and non-surgical orthopedic practices with 1 to 3 locations. We take one clinic per market area, so we never help two practices compete in the same city.
