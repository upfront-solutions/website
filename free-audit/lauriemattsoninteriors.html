What is Domain Rating?

Your website's credit score, or the SEO juice you have banked. Google uses it to decide how much to trust your site.

Three things raise it: publishing content regularly, time online, and other websites linking to yours. Links carry the most weight.

Website Technical Audit

Your site is well designed. Several of the signals Google and AI tools read are missing or pointing at the wrong thing.

Your blog URL has a typo in it

Quick win

Your blog lives at /interior-desgin-blog. "Design" is misspelled in the address itself. Every link, share and search engine sees it. A redirect fixes this in an afternoon.

Your page titles target the state instead of the city

Quick win

Five of your six main page titles say "MN" or "Minnesota". Your customers search "Minneapolis", "St. Paul" and "Twin Cities". On your Minnesota designer page, "Minneapolis" appears only in the footer address.

Schema markup is missing

Needs dev

Schema is hidden code that tells Google: this is a business, here is the address, service area, hours and rating. We found no LocalBusiness or Service markup, and no FAQ markup on your short-term rental page even though those FAQs are already written. This is also what AI tools read when they decide who to name in an answer.

Heading structure is inconsistent

Quick win

Your short-term rental page is set up properly, with a clean H1 of "Airbnb Interior Design". Your Minnesota designer page uses a long H1 with no location in it at all. Google reads the H1 first.

No location pages

Ongoing

There is no page at /interior-designer-minneapolis or /interior-designer-st-paul. Suburb names listed in body text carry far less weight than a full page built for that suburb.

Items that need a full crawl

Needs dev

Image alt text, lazy loading, page speed and internal link depth need a crawl to quantify. Standard Wix housekeeping, all fixable. We would run it in week one.

Same article, same expertise. Only the title changes, to one people are actually searching for. Reworking posts you have already written is the fastest content win available, because the writing is done.

Why keyword titles matter

Writing the way people search helps you in Google, and it also shapes how ChatGPT, Perplexity and AI Overviews decide who to quote. Those tools match a person's question against the wording on your page.

One thing you got right already: your blog is named "interior design blog", which gets 390 searches a month. Strong starting point. It needs optimization and links pointing at it.
